Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

TAAHEEL AI ("we") is a home rehabilitation system for people recovering from a stroke or living with Parkinson's disease, made up of a patient mobile app and a clinician web portal. It is developed as a capstone project at Abu Dhabi University. This policy explains what information we collect, why, who can see it, and the choices you have.

WhatExamplesFrom

2. What we do not collect or keep

  • Camera video is never stored or uploaded. The exercise coach analyses the camera image on your phone; only the measurements leave the device.
  • Voice recordings are not kept. They are sent for analysis, held only while the analysis runs, and then deleted. Only the measurements are stored.
  • We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. How we use information

  • To run the exercise coach, track your progress and show it to you.
  • To let a clinician you have linked with follow your progress and adjust your plan.
  • To produce exercise plan suggestions. Every suggestion is reviewed by your clinician, and nothing in your plan changes without their decision.
  • To send reminders and notifications you can turn off in the app's settings.
  • To keep the service secure, including two-step sign-in for clinicians and an audit trail of access to patient data.

4. Who can see your information

  • You. Patients can see their own records in the app.
  • Clinicians you link with. A clinician sees a patient's information only after the patient accepts their link request, and only while that link is active. A patient can end the link at any time.
  • Administrators manage accounts and verify clinicians. Regulators can review the audit trail and consent records for compliance.
  • Service providers who host the system for us: Supabase (database, sign-in and file storage) and, if you choose it, Google (sign-in). They process information only to provide their service.
  • We may disclose information if the law requires it.

5. Consent

Before using the camera, the microphone or your health information, and before a clinician can see your data, the app asks for your consent. You can withdraw consent in the app at any time. Withdrawing consent for clinician access ends that clinician's link.

6. How we protect information

  • Information is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and stored in a managed database with access rules on every table.
  • Clinicians must use two-step sign-in (an authenticator app).
  • Access to patient data is recorded in a tamper-evident audit log.
  • Backups are taken regularly and kept on encrypted storage.

7. How long we keep information

We keep your information while your account is active and for as long as needed to provide the service and meet legal obligations. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete or anonymise your personal information, except records we must keep, such as the audit trail.

8. Your choices and rights

  • See and correct your profile in the app.
  • Withdraw consents and end clinician links in the app.
  • Turn notifications off in the app's settings.
  • Ask for a copy of your information, or ask us to delete your account, by contacting us (below).

9. Children

TAAHEEL AI is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 18.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
