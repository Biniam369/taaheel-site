Rehabilitation at home, guided by AI and supervised by your clinician.

TAAHEEL AI helps people recovering from a stroke or living with Parkinson's disease do their prescribed exercises at home. A mobile app coaches each movement through the phone's camera, and a secure portal lets their clinician follow progress and adjust the plan.

AI exercise coach

Real-time feedback on posture and movement while the patient exercises. The video is analysed on the phone and never stored.

Progress and assessments

Range of motion, repetitions, compensatory movements and short voice checks, tracked over time against the patient's starting point.

Clinician portal

Clinicians see only the patients who have agreed to link with them, review AI suggestions, and decide every change to the plan.

TAAHEEL AI is a capstone project at Abu Dhabi University and is not yet a certified medical device. It supports, and does not replace, the judgement of a qualified clinician.
